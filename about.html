About

Sebastian had everything an angel could want: status, charm, and a handsome set of wings. Then he lost it all. Cast down to earth with his wings torn, he has one path home: earn back his feathers through acts of goodness. As he embarks on his quest for redemption, he discovers that the line between good and evil are not so well defined.
